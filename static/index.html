FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Aura Skincare — or ask Aria anything.

How long does delivery take?+
Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days from the date of dispatch across India.
Are there shipping charges?+
Delivery is free on orders above ₹499. Orders below ₹499 have a flat ₹50 shipping fee.
What is your return policy?+
Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery for unopened, unused products in their original packaging. Damaged or defective products must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photos for replacement.
Can I cancel my order?+
Orders can be cancelled only while their status is Processing. Once shipped or out for delivery, cancellation is no longer available — you may refuse delivery at the doorstep.
Is Cash on Delivery available?+
Yes. COD is available for orders up to ₹2,500. You can pay by cash or UPI at the doorstep on delivery.
Are Aura products safe for sensitive skin?+
Aura products are formulated to be gentle and suitable for most skin types. Always check the product label for specific suitability and perform a patch test when trying a new product.
